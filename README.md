## What is this?
Welcome to **Blipstorm**!  
A quirky little tower defense game where you’re not the hero. your bots are.  
Defend your island’s core from relentless birds.  
Build, upgrade, and let your bots do the heavy lifting (and shooting).

## Why
Because birds are jerks and automation is cool.  
Also, I wanted to mess around with Godot 4 and Aseprite importers. And island genaration.

## Features
- **Island Genaration** When youre in the Demo press '1' to genarate a new island. (there is a 50/50 change you crash)
- **Bots do the work:** Bots to shoot down birds.
- **Upgrades:** (Coming soon) Make your bots even more awesome.
- **Variety:** (Planned) More bot types, more weapons, more chaos.
